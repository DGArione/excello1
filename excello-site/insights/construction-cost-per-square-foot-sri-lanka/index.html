Construction cost per square foot.

What a per-sqft figure includes, what it leaves out, and how to use the calculator honestly.

A per-square-foot figure is a conversational shortcut. It can be useful when both sides agree on what the figure covers. It is dangerous when either side quietly assumes a different scope.

What a per-sqft figure usually includes

  • Substructure and superstructure
  • Roofing and basic external finishes
  • Standard internal finishes (plaster, floor tiles, ceiling)
  • Standard sanitary ware and basic kitchen fit-out
  • Standard electrical and plumbing

What it rarely includes

  • Land, demolition, boundary walls and site works
  • Upgraded finishes, feature walls, bespoke joinery
  • Air conditioning, solar, back-up power
  • Pools, decks, landscaping and external hard works
  • Statutory fees, utility connections and consultants

How to use the calculator honestly

Pick the type of project, enter a realistic floor area, choose a finish level you can defend and leave the standard assumptions visible. The estimate is a planning number, not a quote. Save it and share it with us so we can discuss what the number is actually buying.
