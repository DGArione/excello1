Aathavan residential project update.

An update on the Aathavan residences, linked to approved facts and verified availability.

Aathavan by Excello is a 44-residence development at No. 6, Carron Place, Dehiwala. The project is in progress; a mix of two- and three-bedroom apartments, with selected three-bedroom units including maid's quarters.

Shared facilities include rooftop gym and outdoor exercise areas, a community hall, a bed-capable lift, solar infrastructure and rainwater-management provisions. The site is close to Kalubowila Teaching Hospital, Dehiwala Railway Station, Marine Drive, schools, supermarkets and the beach.

What is published, and when

Live unit availability and standard pricing are published on the Aathavan availability page and updated when the Unit Register changes. Promotional or negotiated rates may differ.

Project facts, prices and inclusion details are approved separately from this article. The page you are reading is editorial; the authoritative figures sit on the availability page and the project detail.
