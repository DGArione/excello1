Construction cost calculator.

A sequential form that gives you an early-planning estimate for a project in Sri Lanka, with its assumptions on the same screen. It is not a quote.

A range is more honest than a single number: real projects land ±15% of a planning estimate.

Assumptions

  • Rates are for construction only, excluding land, consultants and statutory fees.
  • Finish rates are indicative, reviewed quarterly. Current rates as of this quarter.
  • Air conditioning, solar, pools and external hard works are excluded.
  • Contingency is a sum you plan to spend; carry it in the budget.

Context

An estimate is a starting conversation.

The calculator uses current indicative rates for the Sri Lankan market. Actual cost depends on site conditions, decisions during design and the exact scope. Read our cost-per-square-foot article for what the number does and does not include.
