Who this policy covers

This policy applies to visitors to excello.lk and to people who contact Excello Developers (Pvt) Ltd through an enquiry form, email, WhatsApp or phone.

What we collect

  • Information you give us through enquiry forms: name, contact details, and anything you choose to share about your project.
  • Basic technical data (such as browser and language) from the website.
  • Analytics data (such as pages visited) only where enabled and only in aggregate.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and continue the conversation about your project.
  • To understand what parts of the site are useful, in aggregate.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with unrelated third parties.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept for as long as the conversation is active, plus a reasonable period for follow-up. Project records are kept for the life of the engagement and a period thereafter in line with legal and professional obligations.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask to correct it, or ask us to remove it. Write to inquiry@excello.lk.
