Process

Six everyday steps.

The same six apply whether you are planning a home, a boutique hotel or an apartment block. What changes is their weight and sequence.

01

First conversation

We listen to the aspiration, the site, the budget range and the deadlines. If we are not the right studio for the project, we say so and point to who might be.

A short enquiry is enough to begin. A WhatsApp draft is not a submitted enquiry; actual receipt leads to a responsible person on our side.

02

Site and brief

A written brief and site review. For development enquiries this is the free quick land check, with a clearly limited scope.

The output is a short document that both sides can refer back to.

03

Design direction

Concept drawings, material direction and a cost conversation grounded in current rates. We present options with real trade-offs rather than one hero scheme.

04

Developed design

Drawings and specifications ready to coordinate with structural, services and build teams. Approvals and submissions are coordinated here.

05

Build coordination

Design intent carried on to site. We stay close to the decisions that affect quality and lifetime rather than only those that affect cost.

06

Handover and after

A proper handover with a snag list closed, a defects period, and a studio you can call back when the next decision comes up.
