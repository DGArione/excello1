Panimozhi Club House is a contemporary coastal retreat located close to the beach in Kalkudah, Sri Lanka. Designed with 15 modern rooms, the project offers more than accommodation: a calm, carefully composed setting where architecture, landscape and the surrounding coastal atmosphere work together to support rest and escape.

The design responds to the character of the Eastern Province shoreline through a restrained contemporary language. Natural light, open terraces and subtle textures create a relaxed atmosphere, while the proximity to the beach allows guests to experience the sea breeze, coastal air and sound of the waves as part of the stay.

Each room is planned to combine comfort with a clear connection to its surroundings. The architecture avoids unnecessary complexity, allowing the coastal setting to remain central to the experience.
