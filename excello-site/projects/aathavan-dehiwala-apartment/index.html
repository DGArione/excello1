Aathavan by Excello is a residential apartment development at No. 6, Carron Place, Dehiwala. Set within a quiet neighbourhood close to hospitals, schools, transport links and everyday amenities, the project is planned as a considered urban home for families.

The development brings together 44 residences across a carefully coordinated residential building, with a mix of two-bedroom and three-bedroom homes. Selected three-bedroom apartments include maid's quarters, while each residence is planned with practical family living in mind, including separate kitchens, kitchen balconies and comfortable private spaces.

Context

Located on Carron Place in Dehiwala, Aathavan occupies a well-connected urban site within a quiet residential setting. The project is close to Kalubowila Teaching Hospital, schools, Dehiwala Railway Station, Marine Drive, the beach, supermarkets and other essential amenities.

Design response

The design uses a calm, contemporary architectural language shaped by the scale and character of the surrounding neighbourhood. The apartments are planned to prioritise natural light, ventilation, privacy and efficient daily living.

Shared facilities include a rooftop gym, outdoor exercise areas, a community hall, a bed-capable lift, solar infrastructure and rainwater-management provisions.

Climate strategy

The project responds to Sri Lanka's warm tropical climate through careful planning for daylight, natural ventilation and shaded outdoor areas. Solar infrastructure, rainwater harvesting and treated wastewater considerations reduce resource consumption.

Materials

Warm off-white finishes, natural stone-inspired surfaces, timber tones, textured plaster, glass and matte black aluminium details. A quiet, durable palette that remains practical for everyday family life.
