Our process and people

Six steps, in plain language.

Nothing on this site hides behind jargon. The six steps are the same whether you are planning a home, a boutique hotel or an apartment block; what changes is their weight and sequence.

01

First conversation

We listen to the aspiration, the site, the budget range and the deadlines. If we are not the right studio, we say so.

02

Site and brief

A written brief and site review. For development enquiries, this is the free quick land check.

03

Design direction

Concept drawings, material direction and a cost conversation grounded in current rates.

04

Developed design

Drawings and specifications ready to coordinate with structural, services and build teams.

05

Build coordination

Design intent carried onto site. We stay close to decisions that affect quality and lifetime.

06

Handover and after

A proper handover, a defects period, and a studio you can call back when the next decision comes up.
